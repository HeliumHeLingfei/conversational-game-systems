# Pages AGENTS

Instructions here apply to product-facing pages in `pages/`.

## Focus

- Keep messaging product-centric and clear
- Emphasize concrete capabilities and current development status
- Avoid overly personal framing unless explicitly requested

## Editing Rules

- Preserve valid relative paths (`./style.css`, local links)
- Keep HTML semantic and readable
- Maintain visual consistency with existing dark theme style

## Validation

- Verify contact and GitHub links after edits
- Ensure page sections remain scannable and logically ordered
