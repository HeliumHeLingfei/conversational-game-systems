# Templates AGENTS

Instructions here apply to reusable business templates in `templates/`.

## Focus

- Keep templates reusable and editable
- Preserve placeholder fields for client/project-specific values
- Keep structure professional and print-friendly

## Editing Rules

- Do not hardcode sensitive personal or client data
- Keep invoice/proposal sections explicit and easy to customize
- Avoid removing signature, payment, or scope placeholders unless requested

## Validation

- Check table and section formatting remains intact
- Confirm placeholders are still visible and understandable
